INTERNAL MEMO - CONFIDENTIAL

To: Board of Directors
From: Chief Financial Officer
Date: 4 September 2026
Subject: Third quarter results and hiring freeze

Revenue for the third quarter reached EUR 4.8 million, an increase of twelve percent year over year. The growth was driven mainly by the European expansion, in particular the new customers in Sweden and the Netherlands.

Costs rose faster than planned. The Berlin office hired fourteen engineers instead of the eight approved in the budget, which added roughly EUR 420,000 to quarterly expenses.

Management proposes a hiring freeze for the fourth quarter and will present a revised budget at the November board meeting. We also recommend postponing the Madrid office opening to the second quarter of 2027.

Please treat this document as strictly confidential. Do not forward outside the board.
